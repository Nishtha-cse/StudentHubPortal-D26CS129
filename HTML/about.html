Mission

StudentHub exists to give every student a single, reliable place to manage their academic life — from tracking attendance and assignments to staying on top of campus events — without juggling multiple disconnected tools.

Objectives

Our goals are to simplify everyday academic tasks, improve communication between students and administration, keep everyone informed about campus events, and make it easy to share feedback that leads to real improvements.

Benefits

Students save time by managing everything from one dashboard, staff gain a clearer overview of student activity, and the whole campus community stays better connected through timely announcements and updates.

Features

StudentHub offers attendance tracking, assignment reminders, an events calendar with easy registration, a feedback system, and a personal profile dashboard — all accessible from any device.
